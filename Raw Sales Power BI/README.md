# Raw Sales Analysis Dashboard
## Project Overview
This Power BI solution delivers a comprehensive, interactive Sales Analytics Dashboard designed to transform raw transactional data into actionable business intelligence. Built on a clean data model integrating RAW SALES DATA, PRODUCT LOOKUP and REGION LOOKUP tables, the dashboard provides real-time visibility into sales performance across products, regions, sales teams, customers, and operational metrics.
The following are the key capabilities of the dashboard:
- Executive Overview – High-level KPIs (Total Revenue, Orders, Quantity, Price, and Discount) paired with trend analysis, regional performance, product rankings, order status distribution, and state-level insights.
- Sales Team Performance – Detailed views of revenue and order volume by salesperson, manager contribution, payment method preference, top customers, and product-level discount patterns.
- Interactive filtering across Category, Product, Region, Sales Person, and Payment Method for rapid drill-down analysis.
The dashboard enables leadership and sales managers to quickly identify top-performing regions and team members, monitor revenue trends, evaluate discount effectiveness, track order fulfillment health, and uncover customer and product opportunities, supporting data-driven decision-making and performance optimization.

## Business Problem
Management needs a clear and interactive way to monitor business performance and identify areas that require attention.

The dashboard was designed to answer the following questions:

1.  What is the total revenue, total quantity sold, and total discount given?
2.  What is the average order value and average quantity per order?
3.  How does total revenue break down by Order Status?
4.  What is the monthly or quarterly revenue trend?
5.  Which months or quarters show the highest growth or decline?
6.  Is there any seasonality in sales?
7.  What are the top 10 products by revenue and by quantity sold?
8.  Which products have the highest average discount?
9.  What is the revenue contribution of each product?
10.  Which regions generate the most revenue and quantity?
11.  How does performance differ by State?
12.  Which region has the highest average order value?
13.  Who are the top-performing Sales Persons by revenue and by number of orders?
14.  Which Managers oversee the highest-performing regions?
15.  Is there a big performance gap between the best and worst sales people?
16.  Who are the top customers by total spend?
17.  What is the average number of orders per customer?
18.  Which Payment Methods are used most frequently and which generate the highest revenue?
19.  What is the relationship between Discount % and Order Quantity?
20.  Which products or regions give the highest discounts relative to their revenue?
